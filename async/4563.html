About the work

It all began with a photograph I took 31 years ago. It was one of my first, developed on Ilford film and printed with an old school enlarger. I literally fed the image multiple times to the AI and processed the outcome to what it reminded of me - an island scene lost in time, lost to waves, home to beasts, home to First Ideas. 24 photography based AI driven experimental generative work Life of a sort on an Island Lost to Waves by Hulki Okan Tabak on Async Art
