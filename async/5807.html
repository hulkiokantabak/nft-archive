Monsters & Mythology: Zmaj by Mad Monk 4 pieces minted as 1/1 on Async Art Conceptualized, Created and Minted 2022 ... Zmaj (Zmaj or Dragon) is known in almost all World's cultures. However Zmaj in Slavic folklore is represented as rather benign creature, created from certain animals which lived up to 30 or 100 years (rooster, carp, horse, ox, ram or dog). It always had either one or even number of heads and traits from animal that it borrowed. It fought against Azhdaya and it was invisible to everybody, except to woman whom he falls in love.
