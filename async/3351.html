About the work

Watched by deities older than men, populated in ancient times by the Ptolemaic to the Roman, hosting the brilliant Trireme laden with oils to the fishing dingies of today; the port of Knidos, the mystical harbor that is welcomes human and non-human creatures all alike into its embrace cold and warm at the same time. Photographs from the Summer of 2021 by Hulki Okan Tabak. Conceptualized, edited and minted during September 2021. With a loving heart and a bunch of well intentioned blood sweat and laughter, it is hereby presented in its 24 piece form on Async Art.
