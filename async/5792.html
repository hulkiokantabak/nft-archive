About the work

Monsters & Mythology: Milosnitse by Mad Monk 4 pieces minted as 1/1 on Async Art Conceptualized, Created and Minted 2022 ... Milosnitse are demons of plague, known in all Slavic countries. Their name in English would be merciful ones and Slavs called them like that, because they wanted to appease them and to avoid plague that they bring. They are represented as women in black and they walked in groups, rarely alone. Amount of Milosnitse was measured by amount of sicknesses that they brought. There was no cure from them, but there was a way to appease them and to eventually avoid their plagues.
