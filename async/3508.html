About the work

This is Playing the Card Players from The Most Expensive Artworks Project collaboration by Athena Novo and Mad Monk. It is a remix of Paul Cezanne's The Card Players. It is No 3 in the project in parallel with its place on the list. Accordingly it has been last sold April 2011 in a private sale for USD 250 million. The seller was George Embiricos and the buyer was the State of Qatar. ... The Most Expensive Artworks Project started with an idea and a web page on Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_paintings.
