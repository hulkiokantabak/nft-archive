About the work

She is a series I mint occasionally and She VII 's base image was minted on Foundation at https://foundation.app/@Athena Novo/she-vii-58818. It is an image I've re-imagined from public domain source and I thought a variation piece with Async Art's 24 piece 24 hour template would be a perfect piece to use for variations on the image. I believe the result turned out to be beautiful, interesting and somewhat striking while not being overtly loud. I hope you enjoy the creation as much as I do.
