About the work

AI x Klimt x Adele x Dreams by Hulki Okan Tabak minted on Async Art 24 pieces 24 hours ... This work is based on Gustav Klimt's first portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer (1907). The piece has been sold by Christie's in a private for USD 135 million in 2006. The piece is in public domain. I have walked through various stages of re-imagination on the original piece. I have first applied a general conversion which was subsequently mixed with various genes from randomly selected digital works. I selectively defined starting points and increments that I followed for the next 23 images in journeying with Adele through genders and worlds. The process was awesome, fun and immersive.
