About the work

Dover: Fresco Outside the Earth by Mad Monk Part of a three artwork series on Async Art Minted as a 4-piece artwork on Async Art, 2022 ... The piece is a human AI collaboration based on manipulated text input inspired by Mary Ruefle's poem Seven Postcards from Dover. "The teacher said inner truth and the chalk said like a fresco inside the earth that no one has ever seen and one day decides to be discovered and begins to breathe— do you know what that means?" --- excerpt from Mary Ruefle's poem Seven Postcards from Dover
