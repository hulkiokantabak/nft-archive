About the work

The Ruby Gecko of the Heartland is from a museum trip upon which a set of photographs emerged. It took around 5 years for the images to evolve in to their final form presented here on Async Art. This final form consists of 4 processed photographs that change automatically according to the time of the day. The Ruby Gecko of the Heartland by Hulki Okan Tabak minted 2021 on Async Art
