About the work

"Because I could not stop for Death – He kindly stopped for me – The Carriage held but just Ourselves – And Immortality. ..." --from Because I could not stop for Death by Emily Dickinson Almost Shy is about what is looks like in sunrise in sunset during the day and when it is night..
