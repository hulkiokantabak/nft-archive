About the work

"...I could leave these shores, I could sail home, I could take something with me, I could leave something in return, and at that word it came back, alive." ---from Vapor Wake by Mary Ruefle This is a 2 x 2 x 2 series. 2 states, 2 photographs, 2 artworks. Making Memories and Taking Memories are out of town fun on the road. Photographs are from 1997. As I've read or heard somewhere not that long ago, possibly by Slavoj Zizek or my mind plays with me -- he was saying that memories are meaningful only if photographed.
