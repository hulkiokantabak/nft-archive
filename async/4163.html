About the work

Red Athena Series of 6 packs by Athena Novo, minted 2021 on Async Art The pieces are parts of generative works assisted by artificial intelligence. Portraits are derived from a set of landscape photos and iteratively processed to generate dependent images. Tonality and style is maintained via my control over the process.
