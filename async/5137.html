About the work

38 States of a Young Woman - Set 1 38 States of a Young Woman is a generative study on an image itself created by mixing photography and other generative portraits. There are 38 derivative images of which 28 are used to create 4 sets of images. The sets themselves are created in the order of image creation except for the 3 occasions (out of 28) when an image was replaced by one from the end of the list. There are 7 sets of 4 images Day/Night cycles to be published on Async Art. Athena Novo, 2022
