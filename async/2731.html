About the work

Day begins with Fluid Prophecy where the dancer is enjoying a solo spot under the spotlights. Daylight follows Together under the Spotlights where two lead dancers are in a duet of colorful lights. Night commences with Pull which is taken from the third act and the protagonist is being wound up metaphorically and physically. Rest of the night is the disturbing image of To Break Free with scant hope of escape for the leading woman of the third act. Photography by Hulki Okan Tabak from 2019. Photoshoot from dress rehearsals of a dance project about three women and the hardships they endured. Edited various times with final edit on June 2021 to be minted on Async Art.
