About the work

Bavarian Red Cross by Hulki Okan Tabak minted 2021 on Async Art 4 artworks based on World War I propaganda posters from Germany. In this case Bavarian Red Cross' efforts to raise some money for army. ... The Great War, history, politics, art, posters and propaganda are topics of interest for me. It was kind of natural that I wanted to incorporate that in my cryptoart creations. The desire led to a selection of public domain pieces out of copyright from the Library of Congress archives. I created all sorts of derivative pacifist and tongue-in-cheek works. So far I have only minted a handful on KnownOrigin.
