About the work

4 piece set by Mad Monk, Async Art, 2022 Mad Zombies concept by Mad Monk where unusual zombies engage in all sorts of activities, meet new people, settle old scores and altogether discover what life in the post-apocalyptica. The mints on Async Art are based on Kalahari Zombies where zombies of the south African desert roam wild. The artworks come in 2 piece, 4 piece and 24 piece sets. You can find more of Mad Monk's zombies on Async Art, KnownOrigin and on Mad Monk's own smart contract Mad Monk. https://async.art/u/Mad Monk/collection https://knownorigin.io/mad-monk https://opensea.io/collection/mad-monk-art https://nft.coinbase.com/collection/Mad Monk
