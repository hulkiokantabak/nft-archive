About the work

Bulbasaur 9 series on Async Art is a collaboration between Hulki Okan Tabak and Bulbasaur 9 as a father - son team. Unless extra information is given, all drawings are by B9 and editing is by HoT. Bulbasaur 9 is an emerging artist, YouTuber and storyteller. You can find more about him on his YouTube channel at https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCRjLXhHKyBpI813vFvMiMVQ. This series is a result of library search by the duo. Some of the works were previously minted and burned afterwards. Some of them are minted for the first time.
