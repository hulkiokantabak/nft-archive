About the work

Mobile Houses harks back to 1990s photographs I took with a Pentax SLR. They were re-imagined and processed with AI assistance through guided randomization. When at a certain point the resulting imagery reminded me of small houses, rickety, tentative and afraid of their surroundings; I focused on my selective perception to develop further houses, more places, mobility and chaos on new world, on a an imaginary world.. 24 images, 24 states of mind on Async Art Mobile Houses by Hulki Okan Tabak Minted 2022 on Async Art
