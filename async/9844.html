About the work

“Night City wasn’t there for its inhabitants, but as a deliberately unsupervised playground for technology itself.” ― William Gibson, Neuromancer ... This artwork by Hullki Okan Tabak is part of a the Steampunk Dystopia series. The concept is based on a dark futuristic steampunk location. The artwork is based on the collaboration of photography with guided AI generated images. The series will be minted over time on various platform including Async Art, KnownOrigin and Foundation.
