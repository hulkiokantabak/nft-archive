About the work

Based on Putin photography, Kids Takeover Putin is text-to-image experimentation on war, peace and leadership. It is re-creating the leadership in the eyes of a kid takeover for peace. Stop the War. We hoped, I hoped this was already the past in Europe. We should not go back. There is a lot of interesting material that range from childish and peaceful to satirical and dark. I will post them and perhaps I will post most of them hoping this ends as soon as possible. This is the only 24 piece in this project, I will also be minting 2-piece Nova Apartchik and 4-piece somewhat more abstract pieces on Async Art.
