About the work

This artwork consists of 4 separate digital drawings in a Day/Night cycle as enabled by Async Art's technology. ... Hearing the vocals of Fish when I was 16 was an unforgettable moment. I guess it was Warm Wet Circles the first time and soon after I discovered the early works of Marillion including a set of incredible B sides. Now many a time later, I fed some of the lines from Script for Jester's Tear to the VQAN+CLIP software. I experimented with the results with additions, my photographs and some iterations to arrive at these images. The final edit in Photoshop was only for sizing and minor contrast curve adjustment. The artwork is pretty much AI x HoT as it came out. ... Water…
