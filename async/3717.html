About the work

Magic Man lived a long time ago in a place far far away. Magic Man wore a hat that housed his powers. Never took it off for fear of losing it. When he got too old to perform magic, Magic Man left his home in search of an apprentice. During his travels, Magic Man encountered a world unknown to him. He loved and loathed what he saw. He desired and cried incessantly. His emotions were mixed, his magic wat patchy, his hat was ragged. I've met him during his travels. It was by the inn on the High Steppes that he was so tired that he fell asleep on foot when he stumbled in. I helped the keep carry him to a room that was to be his last.
