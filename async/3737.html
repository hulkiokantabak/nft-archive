About the work

Why Do Words Return? When you find it Is it because of the search Or the pretense of a search? What you find Is it what you sought for? Or is the search on because you already found? December 2012 — June 2020 ... Why Do Words Return dates back many years ago as a poem in Turkish (2012) which I translated into English to publish on Medium (2020). The photographs accompanying the text are from a 2020 nature macro shoot. This NFT Art version minted by Hulki Okan Tabak, September 2021 on Async Art
