About the work

24 collaborative drawings in a single artwork that changes by the hour. Devilish Thieves and Sad Teardrops is a collaboration between Bulbasaur 9 and Hulki Okan Tabak. The primary drawing comes from a personal project by B9 and HoT has expanded on the subject matter with different degrees and methods of reimagination. There are three 24 piece collaborations as a part of this project which will be all minted on Async Art. The drawings are from October 2021, reimagination took place in November 2021 and expected time of minting is early 2022. Meanwhile collaboration also created GIFs based on individual stills and they will be minted on other cryptoart platforms.
