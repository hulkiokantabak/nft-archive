About the work

Lady Luck from Manifesto of Chance - III Manifesto of Chance is a Blueprints project I've created involving trillions of combinations. Its ultimate "massage" is to let go and seize the day. Lady Luck series is derived from one of the base images used in the project and it is extensively edited. They are stand alone pieces which point out to seeing (not solving) the larger puzzle of the project. None of the Lady Luck themed artworks appear in the project as the project is rather strange and different and wild and crazy. conceptualized, created and minted by Athena Novo on Async Art 2022
