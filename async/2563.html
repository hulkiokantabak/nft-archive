Scenes from the Show

by Hulki Okan Tabak · Async Art Master #2563 · lever-controlled · time rule: UTC

lever-controlled: 9 layers set by their holders' levers

Each layer is its own token; whoever holds it sets its lever. The Master shows what they choose.

Shuffle: 68,719,476,736 possible combinations (16 × 16 × 16 × 16 × 16 × 16 × 16 × 16 × 16: the options of its 9 lever layers, multiplied). It stacks the layers in order, as our composite does, and gives each lever layer a random option about every 5 seconds, the fixed layers kept; each picture it shows is captioned “A possible combination — not the current on-chain state”.

About the work

Scenes from the Show is from a show about three women who have been hurt, reacted and consequently suffered. Their efforts were not in vain but not all were able to survive the ordeal. I should, with the blockchain as my witness, thank my daughter for being a part of this show and facilitating my presence as a photographer on site. The photographs have been taken during the rehearsals in 2019. I had the opportunity to take a wide variety of shots out of which I have handpicked a few for Scenes from the Show. The photographs were edited multiple times during the period leading up to this artwork. The show is about their courage. It is about their will not to be broken under adverse…
