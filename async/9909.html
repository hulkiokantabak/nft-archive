About the work

"In the United terminal in Chicago at five on a Friday afternoon The sky is breaking with rain and wind and all the flights Are delayed forever. We will never get to where we are going And there’s no way back to where we’ve been. The sun and the moon have disappeared to an island far from  anywhere. Everybody has a heartache — " ---from Everybody Has a Heartache: A Blues by Joy Harjo ... Surreal Storyteller is stranded in his own stories. He can not get to where he is going as that place is constructed via the story he tells. Lacking the necessary audience, Surreal Storyteller is stuck in a semi-permanent existence of de-construction and re-construction amidst his stories. ... Surreal…
