About the work

"Once there were people here. More people than you'd ever believe. More people than Earth has seen since the day or.. Or even until now. They lived in this huge tower. Kind of like the massive multi purpose high rises they have in China these days. But bigger as there was not exactly an end in sight. I mean if you looked at it it was a structure. Defined with boundaries and all that. However when you tried to find the end of, the last floor for example, it was an effort in vain. In The Old Tower, the end as well as the beginning were all elusive, at best an ouroboros." --excerpt from Interviews with Walter, A Mindread by J.J.F. Nokihufo ...
