About the work

The imaginary soldiers of Athena Novo.. Screwbots do not fight well if they fight at all. But they do love a role play, hide and seek, imitation game and what not. In this definitive Screwbots piece we get to see them in Athena's vision during the day. What a bore! Screw them all!! Screwbots by Athena Novo, minted on Async Art, August 2021
