6 works · 18 files: for each work our copies of its record, its image (the preview) and its live view (the page that draws the art in a browser), each pinned at Pinata and Filebase and archived in S3. A marketplace reading the token's address goes to Art Blocks' servers, not to these copies; the copies keep the record, preview and live view if those servers stop, and their fingerprints are in the public list (see how these works are kept and proven). Artists are credited as the works' own records name them.
