Async Art — living works: the 342 Async Art works by Hulki Okan Tabak, Athena Novo and Mad Monk, shown live — every state side by side, the state for the current time, and the layers each is built from.

How these works are kept and proven (in one picture) — the three keepers, the labels, the fingerprints, the witnesses and the Bitcoin timestamps.

This is a preservation archive. The works shown with a saved file had their files on a host that has since died, or on a centralized server, or on an IPFS address that no node provided when checked twice through IPFS routing on 2026-09-22, and that OpenSea's IPFS gateway no longer served. A copy of what could still be retrieved was saved and pinned at Pinata; each saved file is given with its content identifier (CID) and links to three public IPFS gateways, in the order to try them, together with where it came from and when, its length in bytes and its sha256. Each file is stored on IPFS under its CID. The CID belongs to the file, not to any website, so other IPFS gateways can open it too. Three that served our files on 2026-11-18: gateway.pinata.cloud, ipfs.filebase.io, c-ipfs-gw.nmkr.io. To check a saved file, download it by its CID from an IPFS gateway and compare its length and sha256 with the ones given here (they are also in archive_index.json). Works whose files are on IPFS and pinned, or fully on-chain, need no saved file; each collection page lists the held ones on IPFS in a table under the cards, and the works stored on Arweave with our own IPFS copy as a second source. Artists are credited as named in the works' own metadata. Nothing here is offered for sale or as a substitute for the original token.

Each work has one or more saved files: the image or video, and the metadata record.
